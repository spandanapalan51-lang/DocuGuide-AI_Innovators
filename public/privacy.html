Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. About this prototype

DocuGuide AI is a student educational website. It does not connect to DigiLocker accounts and cannot retrieve or view users' documents.

2. login

The login asks for a display name. If browser session storage is available, the name is kept in that browser session to personalize the troubleshooting page. They send the name to a project server. It create or verify an account.

3. Troubleshooting messages

The current assistant uses sent to an AI service by this website. Avoid entering personal or sensitive information into the assistant.

4. Contact form

The contact form prepares an email draft in your default email application. It does not submit the message to a DocuGuide server. Your email application and email provider may handle the draft under their own policies.

5. Sensitive information

Never enter passwords, OTPs, full identity numbers or private documents into the website. Use official DigiLocker channels for account-specific help.

6. Third-party resources

The stylesheet references Google Fonts. If your browser loads those fonts, the request may be handled by Google under its applicable policies. The website can use fallback fonts if the service is unavailable.

7. Future updates

This policy describes the current website. If authentication, analytics, databases, AI services or other integrations are added, this policy should be reviewed and updated to reflect the actual data practices.

8. Contact

For website feedback, visit the Contact page.

Terms & Conditions

Last updated: October 2026

1. Purpose of the project

DocuGuide AI is a student project prototype that demonstrates general troubleshooting guidance for DigiLocker document retrieval issues.

2. Not an official service

DocuGuide AI is an independent project. It is not an official DigiLocker service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by a government department. It does not verify identities, access government records or issue documents.

3. Use the prototype safely

Do not enter passwords, one-time passwords (OTPs), full identity numbers or private documents into the prototype. Use official websites and support channels for account-specific help.

4. Informational guidance

The guidance is general and may not apply to every document, issuer or situation. Confirm important instructions through official DigiLocker help or the relevant issuing authority.

5. No guarantee

This prototype cannot guarantee that a document will be found or that an issue will be resolved. It does not connect to a user's DigiLocker account.

6. Changes to the prototype

Features and content may change as the student project develops. Availability of the prototype is not guaranteed.

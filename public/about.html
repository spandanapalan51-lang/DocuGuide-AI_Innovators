ABOUT THE PROJECT

Making document troubleshooting easier.

DocuGuide AI is a student project prototype designed to help users understand common issues when trying to retrieve documents through DigiLocker.

Our problem statement

Users may have difficulty retrieving documents when they encounter a “Document not found” error. They may not know the reason for the error or the appropriate steps to resolve it.

Our goal

Our goal is to provide clear, step-by-step troubleshooting guidance that helps users identify possible causes and determine an appropriate next action. The project covers marksheets, certificates, IDs and other documents where relevant.

Our key features

  • Simple explanations of common document retrieval issues.
  • Step-by-step checks for possible causes.
  • Suggestions for the next appropriate action.
  • A user-friendly interface with clear navigation.

What this website cannot do

It cannot sign in to DigiLocker, access private records, fetch documents, or guarantee that an issue will be resolved. Its current troubleshooting assistant uses sample responses.

DocuGuide AI is an independent educational website and is not affiliated with or endorsed by DigiLocker or any government department.
